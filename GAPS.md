# Remaining proof obligations

1. **Internal semantics:** define actual models of ZF, internal functions and relations, internal well-orders, names, forcing and generic extensions. Host `WO`, `PP`, `SVC`, `OrdIndex` and choice-valued arguments do not provide this interpretation.
2. **First kernels:** supply image closure from model Separation/Replacement and the powerset object from the model's Powerset axiom. `StageCapture` already proves capture from its explicit premises; proper-class indexing is not supplied.
3. **Choice:** derive internal ordinal selectors and use the model's AC for well-ordered families. The countable-block witness remains explicit. In ZF, countable choice *does* imply that every infinite set has a countably infinite subset; this derivation is missing code, not a mathematical counterexample. The Pincus implication PP implies AC_WO is not formalized.
4. **Repair:** construct concrete swap/bit/block coding and freshness instances; prove full order-projection lifting; provide genuine generic-filter semantics. `Nat -> Bool` is the ambient real type, not a coded collection of old-model reals. A common-universe implication with genericity premises cannot certify a real-collapse extension. Sigma closure, DC preservation, no-new-reals and the Feferman model application are absent.
5. **Class obstruction:** assemble SVC slicing, new-trace production and actual transitive model chains. `StageFreeze` packages incompatible freezing/freshness assumptions and its elimination is only a logical lemma. It does not construct the class iteration. Static-product, thin-target and Scott-type variants are not implemented.
6. **Cohen:** construct complete Boolean algebras/subalgebras, Borel representatives, splitting, accelerated two-colouring, delayed domains, name transport and mixing. `rank_contra` merely rejects its assumed rank two-cycle; it does not establish these premises from LC/PP.
7. **Random:** construct the measure algebra, random names and the probability counterexample. `CategoryAcc` has only a colour and a well-founded relation; absence of an instance is not a negative theorem or test.
8. **Fuchs-Prikry:** implement actual ideals, almost-inclusion, support translation, automorphism groups, normal filters and hereditary symmetry. `criterion_forward` only transports a supplied well-order along a supplied surjection and does not depend on `PosIdeal` or `IdealMax`. No support-ideal equivalence is proved. DC implies countable choice, but the general converse is false; a converse in a special model needs its precise additional assumptions.

The files compile as conditional statements in Lean; these gaps prevent claiming the motivating model theorems are fully formalized.
